## Third-party technology

The execution platform is commercially licensed third-party technology. This case does not claim authorship of:

- the platform itself;
- its native execution engine;
- its GUI;
- its built-in metrics or reports;
- third-party documentation or platform internals.

I personally owned:

- requirements discovery and technical-commercial framing;
- product R&D and target-system design;
- external control and operations architecture;
- the MCP-based execution-platform integration boundary;
- quantitative validation and trading-system development;
- Python data/AI and operational components;
- testing, deployment and production operations;
- the surrounding Proxmox/Hetzner/Linux/container environment;
- incident work, client communication and reporting.

## Confidential implementation

The public case does not establish publication rights over client code, source code, functional specifications or implementation artifacts.

Exact commands, schemas, mappings, configuration logic, control flow, code and strategy logic remain confidential even when their production existence is stated publicly.

Public links to third-party community contributions require verified authorship, public accessibility and written permission for any provider-name or brand reference.

Private-user trading-platform consulting is described only at capability level. It does not establish publication rights over a client's strategy, code, data, results or communications.
