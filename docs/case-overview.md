# Case overview

## Business and engineering context

A private trading-systems engagement required continuous ownership across quantitative validation, automated execution, external operational control, infrastructure reliability and client reporting.

HaasOnline TradeServer was used as a third-party execution core. It became the first proven connector through an MCP-based integration boundary. A confidential external AI/TradeOps control and operations layer was designed, implemented and operated around that boundary.

The connector and surrounding workflows were validated in laboratory and production environments.

The underlying evidence pack spans more than 11,900 tested bot configurations/candidates and 589 selected candidates across documented reporting periods. These figures describe operational scale; this repository makes no investment-performance claim.

## Scope demonstrated

- quantitative research-to-production;
- structured strategy review and selection at scale;
- repeatable laboratory, historical and SIM/paper validation;
- risk visibility, controlled review and decision evidence;
- modular algorithmic trading systems;
- Python data and AI services;
- external integration and operational control;
- multi-server Proxmox/Hetzner operations;
- Linux, containers, networking and reliability;
- testing, deployment, incident work and reporting;
- technical discovery and long-term client ownership.

## Evidence model

This public repository provides black-box evidence. It establishes what was built, the production status, the third-party boundary and my role.

It does not disclose how the protected implementation works internally.
