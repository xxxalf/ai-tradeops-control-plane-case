# Disclosure policy

## L0 — Public

Audience: GitHub, LinkedIn, CVs, job applications and cold outreach.

Allowed:

- outcomes and capability classes;
- production status and time period;
- personal role and third-party boundary;
- high-level technology inventory;
- HTS as the first proven MCP-based connector;
- evidence categories and references.
- operating-scale counts only when their source and counting method can be
  explained; they are never presented as investment performance.

## L1 — Controlled hiring

Audience: confirmed recruiter, hiring manager or interview panel.

Allowed through redacted screen-share:

- selected contribution and test evidence;
- high-level engineering decisions;
- sanitized chronology and operating context.

No protected production/client source, schemas, commands or implementation packages are transferred. Separately owned and sanitized evaluation samples may be shared outside this repository when directly relevant to the role.

## L2 — Qualified B2B

Audience: identified decision-maker with a qualified problem.

Condition: confidentiality agreement and explicit meeting scope.

Allowed:

- limited problem-specific capability mapping;
- evidence sufficient to support a paid next step.

No reusable blueprint is provided.

## L3 — Paid delivery

Audience: contracted client team.

Condition: NDA, MSA/SOW, IP and licensing terms, payment or funded milestone.

Allowed:

- detailed discovery;
- target-connector assessment;
- client-specific architecture;
- implementation and validation plan;
- contracted deliverables.

## Always confidential

- commercial proposal and functional specification;
- detailed architecture and control/data flow;
- MCP tools, commands and sequences;
- JSON schemas, mappings and payloads;
- configuration logic and operational algorithms;
- source code and executable components;
- client reports, logs and infrastructure identifiers;
- proprietary strategy logic and security mechanisms.

Historical return figures, recovery estimates and individual account outcomes
are also excluded from this public repository. If they are ever relevant to a
qualified private discussion, they require primary evidence, context and an
explicit non-predictive disclaimer.
